# Five Seconds to Explain the Glass

The one sentence a house cat says in its own defence, five seconds after sweeping a glass off the table. No apology, no confession — just the most convincing case a cat can make for itself: the glass was the trespasser.

## Entry

> The glass was squatting in my sunbeam, paying no rent and blocking my view, so I evicted it — the floor just handled the paperwork.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the single sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
